01 / THE RUNTIME

HUMAN INTENT. GAME AUTHORITY.

THE STORY IS OPEN.
THE RULES ARE YOURS.

A player wants to rescue a friend without harming the creature in the way. Your game already has the actions. Runtime Story connects that intention to a plan your game can actually execute.

01

Describe your actions, state and constraints. Keep the mechanics, resources and success conditions in ordinary game code.

02

MAKE ROOM FOR INTENT

OpenAI composes registered actions into a candidate plan. The runtime checks schemas and simulates its consequences.

planIntent()
