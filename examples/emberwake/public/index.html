    Intent → plan → playable consequences.

    You describe an outcome and constraints. Live OpenAI selects from nine registered mechanics. The unchanged Runtime Story SDK simulates every step against the actual RPG state. Review the terms before accepting.

    You still play. Every step changes real health, inventory, enemies, companions or travel. Choose a different action and the tool must replan around it. The storm is an authored event. The replacement sequence is generated.

    Without inference, all manual RPG controls still work. What disappears is interpreting an unfamiliar request and composing a custom checked route. There is no hidden library of complete routes and no automatic invention of new mechanics.
